# G2 results — M1/M1b kernel ridge, R1, T, modality ablation, M2 (dev CV)

## Summary and interpretation (written after all runs; numbers are from the tables below)

**Selection (pre-registered rule, DESIGN §4).** M1 on expression only (`m1_E`) is selected:
P1 variable 0.2997 ± 0.0067, ΔP1 vs B1 +0.116 (SE 0.004), vs B2 +0.047 (SE 0.002), 5/5 folds.
No M1b modality set beats it, and no M2 variant clears δ = 0.005. M2 + paralogs reaches
+0.0048 (SE 0.0003), just below δ. **Selected model after G2: `m1_E`.**

**1. With default 1/√p_b block weights, every add-one modality hurts.**
- The losses are C −0.013, M −0.007, H −0.024, F −0.019, composites −0.008 and Meta −0.000.
  The full set loses 0.029.
- Equal total weight per modality puts a few dozen hotspot or fusion columns on the same kernel
  scale as about 18k expression genes. Those columns are then nearly unpenalised, and the shared
  λ doubles.
- Leave-one-out from the full set does show signal: dropping C costs 0.009 and dropping E costs
  0.021 (both Holm p < 0.001, 5/5 folds). Dropping H gains 0.012.
- So this ablation answers "does the modality help under default weights?" (no). It does not
  answer "does the modality carry signal?" (C clearly does).
- The deferred M1b weight tuning (DESIGN §5: coordinate descent on the training LOO, ≤25 eigh)
  is the obvious next step before any claim that genomics adds nothing.
- The C common-subset rule (967 C-covered lines) gives the same conclusions: add C −0.013;
  C in full +0.010.

**2. The pre-registered B1-residual target costs about 0.008 (diagnostic; not a selection
candidate).**
- With identical features, λ grid and NaN handling, a B0-residual target gives 0.3081:
  ΔP1 vs M1-E +0.0084 (SE 0.0017), 5/5 folds.
- T (E + TP53/RB1 flags, no lineage) gives 0.3082 and R1 (raw y, single λ) gives 0.3073.
- The TP53/RB1 flags add nothing (+0.0001). Grouped vs single λ adds +0.0008 (n.s.).
- Interpretation: a fixed, unit-weight shrunk lineage mean is a worse offset than lineage
  information learned from expression.
- Switching M1's target is a design change and needs a PI decision. Under the current rule, the
  transfer model T is the best-scoring E-only model.

**3. M2 local stage.**
- Small gains on variable genes: none +0.0026, paralogs +0.0048, partners +0.0040.
- Paralogs vs none: +0.0022 (Holm p < 0.01). Partners vs paralogs: −0.0008.
- P1 on *all* genes drops from 0.150 to 0.141–0.147, and on non-essential genes from 0.110 to
  0.098. The per-gene stage overfits low-variance genes.
- A pass-through of M1 for non-variable genes is a cheap fix to try.

**4. Controls and things that look too good.**
- Permuted-feature negative control: λ goes to the upper grid edge (1e7) and the predictions
  reproduce B1 exactly (ΔP1 = 0.0000). This passes.
- The provenance arm (GrowthPattern/ModelType/PrimaryOrMetastasis) adds +0.014 to the full model
  (5/5 folds). This is a batch/library proxy, as the design anticipated. It must not be used.
- Hart NEGv1 non-essential genes reach P1 0.110 with M1 (B1 0.068). Biologically this should be
  about 0, so part of the "skill" is line-level technical structure, such as screen
  quality or library, that can be predicted from expression and lineage. It is worth a
  within-NaN-group / per-line-centred check before headline claims.
- P subset (253 lines, ~200 per training fold): E+P −0.006 (Holm p 0.11). The "other" λ hits
  the lower grid edge (100) in some folds. This arm is underpowered.

**5. Runtime caveats.**
- Fit times are sums over 5 folds, including inner κ tuning and the LOO grid.
- They were measured with 6 or 12 BLAS threads (see table). The machine was shared with other
  agents' jobs (a Chronos fit, transfer scripts, and an independent re-run of 19 of these configs).
- Treat cross-run runtime differences below about 1.5× as noise. M1-E takes about 100 s per fold
  at 12 threads. M2 takes 11–20 min per fold: the inner M1 fits are cached and shared across
  prior variants.
- The 19 duplicated configs give bit-identical P1. B1 and B2 re-runs reproduce G1 exactly.
  B2 vectorisation cut fit time from 2108 s to 219 s.
