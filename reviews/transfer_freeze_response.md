(written by `scripts/transfer_criteria.py`), `src/essml/transfer.py` and `tests/test_transfer.py` (26 tests).

those reads are logged as `replicate_ceiling_aggregate`.

The computed ceilings (2000 resamples) are in `configs/transfer_criteria.json → backgrounds.<bg>.replicate_ceiling`.
Per-entry aggregates are in `results/harmonisation/transfer_ceiling_entries.csv`; bootstrap replicates are in
`transfer_ceiling_bootstrap.csv`. The pre-D13 `transfer_ceiling_pairs.csv` has been deleted.

**Points for the PI.**
1. The replicate ceiling barely beats B0 in RPE1 (Q3a 0.031; Q3b 0.002) and is negative in hPSC (−0.098 and −0.065). The 50%-of-ceiling condition is therefore near-vacuous in RPE1 and vacuous in hPSC. As written, "transfers" is effectively "lower CI > 0". It is applied literally and flagged.
2. Resampling the predictor pools inflates the MDE: RPE1 Q3a 0.101, against 0.020 with fixed pools (sensitivity stored). A larger MDE makes "fails" easier to reach.
3. Brown WT and Brown null are treated as one study for resampling and LOSO, so the RPE1 background has 4 resampling units, not 5.
